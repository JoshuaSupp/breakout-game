How to play

Smash every brick before the clock runs out. The ball waits on your paddle until you launch it, and the clock starts on your first launch. Each stage gets faster, with more rows and less time.

Hit bricks back-to-back without touching the paddle to build a combo. Every 3 hits in a row raise your score multiplier (watch the COMBO meter on the dashboard), up to x8. Bricks with rivets are armored and take more than one hit. Every stage you clear puts +1 life and your leftover time into your bonus bank. Banked time is capped at 10 minutes. It is saved, and you can spend it on any stage you pick from the Get Ready screen, or keep it for later.

After stages 15 and 30 an alien boss attacks. Hit it with the ball until its hull hits zero, and dodge its red plasma shots: each one that reaches your paddle costs a life.
